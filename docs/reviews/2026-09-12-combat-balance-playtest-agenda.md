# Separate human balance playtest

Automated state/chronology checks do not establish fun, fairness, visual clarity, or live latency.
This agenda is separate from native release certification and authorizes no deployment.

- Compare perfect, ordinary, and late shield timing over the real connection; include hazard
  parry, reduced knockback, held-Space cancellation, and fresh input after stun.
- Try repeated charge at zero drag and deployed drag, cooldown timing, the initially selected
  20,000 wu/s safety envelope, and recovery near cliffs. Record whether a shove feels earned.
- Compare Keeper, Bully, Opportunist, and Cautious Racer on identical maps. Distinguish tuning
  from the owed stable-interior, recovery-path, and ordinary-thrust shove mechanisms. Arrival
  braking's overshoot contract is a release-review decision, not a balance result.
- Judge cliff/void readability, the gold perfect rings, blue ordinary ring, violet stun marks,
  and visibility when the body falls or leaves the viewport.
- Try KeyS/KeyD alongside Space and camera panning. Record charge's current mouse dependence;
  touch, pen, and keyboard-only charge remain a known input limitation.
- Complete live race fairness and finish-window playtests separately from exact replay chronology.
